What SwarLekh does

Every feature is built around one goal: remove the friction from examinations for institutions, teachers and students.

📝

Question paper creation

Build papers from a question bank with marks, difficulty and topic balance. AI can suggest questions for a syllabus unit.

💻

Online exam delivery

Students take exams on a clean, simple screen with timers, autosave and clear instructions.

🗂️

Answer management

Answers are stored safely and organised by exam, batch and student, ready for evaluation.

✅

Evaluation support

Rubrics and AI suggestions help teachers mark faster and more consistently.

Prajakta Prabhakar Pare

Founder & Team Lead, SwarLekh

Building a smarter and more inclusive future for education.

I am the founder and team lead of SwarLekh, an initiative focused on transforming the way educational institutions manage examinations. My vision is to simplify the examination lifecycle through technology, reduce repetitive administrative work for educators, and create more accessible assessment experiences for students.

Through SwarLekh, I aim to address the challenges faced by colleges and universities in question paper creation, examination delivery, answer management, evaluation, and result processing.

A key part of this vision is supporting visually impaired students who often face difficulties finding scribes during examinations. By exploring accessible technologies such as screen-reader compatibility, text-to-speech, and voice-based answer input, SwarLekh aims to help students participate more independently.

I believe technology should not only make processes faster but also create equal opportunities. Through continuous learning, collaboration, and innovation, I aspire to make SwarLekh a reliable assessment platform for educational institutions across India.

My mission: To make examinations smarter, simpler, and more inclusive for every learner.
